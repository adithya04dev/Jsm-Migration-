# Context and motivation

## Problem
The existing incident alerting workflow used PagerDuty, and its license was approaching expiry. The work aimed to migrate the workflow to Jira Service Management (JSM).

## Why it matters
The presentation should explain continuity of alert delivery, escalation, and on-call response during migration. These are the intended operational concerns, not independently verified performance outcomes.

## Intended outcome
Replace the relevant PagerDuty workflows with JSM integrations and preserve incident-response capability.

## Scope caveat
This is a high-level summary for a public presentation handoff. Exact architecture, environments, integrations, dates, owners, and commercial terms are deliberately omitted.
