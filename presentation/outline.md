# Peer review presentation outline

Target: approximately 5–6 slides plus an approved live demo.

1. **Title & objective** — PagerDuty to JSM migration overview.
2. **Problem & motivation** — License expiry and need for operational continuity.
3. **Before / after flow** — Conceptual Grafana → PagerDuty versus Grafana → JSM; label this conceptual unless an approved diagram is available.
4. **Implementation** — Grafana contact point, alert routing, on-call schedules and escalation policies.
5. **Validation & readiness** — Present verified test outcomes from internal records; mention remaining team actions only if still open.
6. **Summary & next steps** — Benefits, any outstanding cutover tasks, and retirement status (verify before presenting).

1. Show the approved Grafana contact point configuration.

3. Explain how on-call schedules and escalation policies apply.
